About Me

I tend to study machine learning systems as complex systems whose behavior emerges from the interaction of data, architectures, and training paradigms. Historically, my work has focused on understanding failure modes, logic traps, explanations, and privacy in contemporary ML paradigms. More broadly, I am nowadays interested in emergent behavior in these systems and AI safety. I'm fortunate to work with Jordan & Kaiqing, and am currently affiliated with the University of Maryland, College Park.

Outside of work, I mostly read Rumi and Kabir.
